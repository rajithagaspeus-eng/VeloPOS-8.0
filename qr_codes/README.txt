YOUR OWN QR PICTURES  (shown on the Activate screen and the About screen)
=========================================================================
Put your QR pictures in THIS folder (qr_codes, next to run.py) with exactly these names.
Any picture type works: .png  .jpg  .jpeg  .gif  .webp  .bmp   (a square picture is best; crop the QR tight, white border is fine)

    qr_international.png   ->  "International (Card)" tab   (e.g. your Gumroad / card payment QR)
    qr_bank.png            ->  "Local Bank" tab             (e.g. your bank's LankaQR / Sampath payment QR)
    qr_binance.png         ->  "Binance Pay" tab            (Binance app -> Pay -> Receive -> your QR)
    qr_whatsapp.png        ->  "WhatsApp" tab               (your WhatsApp "QR code" from WhatsApp -> Settings)
    qr_about.png           ->  the QR on the About & License screen  (if it is missing, qr_whatsapp.png is used there)

NOTE: the four payment tabs (Card / Bank / Binance / WhatsApp) are on the ACTIVATE screen, which only appears on a copy that is not activated yet.

* A picture that is not there  =  the program draws a QR from the text instead (the old behaviour). You can add them one at a time.
* Close VeloPOS and open it again after adding / changing a picture.
* This folder goes INSIDE the zip you give to customers, so they see your QR codes. (It contains only pictures, nothing secret.)
* Test each one by scanning it with your phone from the screen.
